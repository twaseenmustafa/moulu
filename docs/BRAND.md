# BRAND — MOULU

## Name
| Candidate | Check (web search, Nov 2026) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Ollo | Knoll "Ollo" chair + "Ollo Home" furniture line | rejected — taken in furniture |
| Orvo / Embra / Sulo | existing ottoman / sofa / healthcare-sofa product names | rejected |
| **Moulu** | no furniture or sofa brand found (only the French word and "ormolu") | **chosen** |
| Halcyo | too close to "Halcyon" brands, harder to spell | backup |

**Why Moulu:** *mou* is French for "soft"; *or moulu* (ormolu) is the old craft of copper-gold gilding — softness + copper, the exact two ideas of the product and the palette. Two syllables, "moo-loo", same in DE/FR/EN. The round letters (M O U L U) behave like cushions in a giant wordmark, so the sofa can sink into the letters.

## One-line idea
**Furniture you sink into, slowly.** A sofa built around one measured feeling: the 9 cm sink.

## Voice
Warm, unhurried, precise. Short sentences. Numbers instead of adjectives. Never "luxury", "elevate", "curated". A little dry humour ("Built for the long sit.").

## Market (assumption)
EU, prices in EUR incl. VAT, free white-glove delivery in DE/AT/NL/BE/FR, 8–10 week make-to-order. Launch model: **waitlist + free reservation** (no payment taken). *Assumed because the market placeholder was not filled.*

## Brand story (fictional — placeholder)
> *Placeholder story, written for the concept site.*
Moulu began as an argument about a feeling. Two people, one too-firm sofa, and a question nobody in the showroom could answer: how far should you sink? We measured. Nine centimetres — enough to be held, not enough to be swallowed. Every Moulu piece is built backwards from that number: frame first, then springs, then three foams, then feathers, then the fabric that gets to say hello.

## Logo / wordmark
Custom SVG wordmark: geometric, heavy, rounded terminals; the two **U**s are drawn as cushion forms (soft bottoms, slightly bulged sides). The **O** is a perfect circle used alone as the brand mark (favicon). Delivered in `public/brand/` and inline in the site.
