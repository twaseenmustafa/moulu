006 — Why Moulu (fictional brand story)

It started as an
argument about a feeling.

Two people, one too-firm sofa, and a question nobody in the showroom could answer: how far should you sink? So we measured. Nine centimetres — enough to be held, not enough to be swallowed.

Mou is French for soft. Or moulu is the old craft of gilding with ground copper-gold. Softness and copper — that’s the whole brand, in one word.

Specs

Questions,
answered.

What does reserving actually do?

It holds a place in the first production run. You pay nothing now. Before we cut your fabric we email you to confirm or cancel — no answer, no order.

Can I feel the fabric first?

Yes. Every reservation gets a free sample of the fabric you picked, posted within two weeks. Swap fabrics as often as you like before production.

What does “9 cm sink” mean?

Place 75 kg in the centre of a seat cushion and measure how far the top drops: 9 cm. Enough to be held, not enough to struggle getting up. Crumb is firmer at 6 cm, because feet go on it too.

Delivery and returns?

Free white-glove delivery to Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Belgium and France, carried in and unwrapped. 30-day return window from delivery. (Planned terms — placeholder.)

How do I clean it?

Every cover zips off and can be dry-cleaned. Spare covers will be sold separately, so a sofa can change mood without changing sofa.
